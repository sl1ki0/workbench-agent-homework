# Presentation outline

1. Clarify the audience and what decision or action the audience should take.
2. Draft the slides in a sequence that develops one point per slide.
3. For each slide, suggest a concise title, the supporting fact or example, and a compatible visual treatment.
4. Check that the opening frames the audience's question and the final slide gives an actionable conclusion.
