---
name: study-note-checklist
description: Turn a study note into a concise revision checklist with concrete next steps and questions.
---

1. Read the requested study note.
2. Identify the concepts, unresolved questions and observable next steps stated in the note.
3. Save a Markdown checklist with one action per row in the requested path.
4. Mark unanswered questions explicitly and avoid inventing answers or sources.
5. Read the saved checklist back and confirm it is grounded in the note.
